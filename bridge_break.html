HOW TO PLAY

1. Pick a material, click a green anchor or any spot, click again to place a piece. Chain pieces to build. Right-click ends the chain.

2. Only deck pieces (Wood Plank, Steel Deck) carry vehicles. Beams, cables and concrete hold the deck up.

3. Press TEST BRIDGE. Vehicles drive over. Watch the stress colours: green → yellow → orange → red = about to break.

Engineering: triangles are rigid · cables only pull · long compression members buckle · shorter spans are stronger · heavier vehicles need stronger decks.
